Visit Our Communities

  • Ravenswood, West Virginia

    Four historic 1890s cottages along the Ohio River. Experience small-town hospitality with walkable downtown, river views, and front porch conversations. Stay with us as a short-term guest or inquire about longer-term community living.

  • Parkersburg, West Virginia

    Mid-century modern homes featuring the largest victory garden in the city. Urban homesteading meets contemporary design in West Virginia's growing arts district. Perfect for remote workers and creatives.

  • Marietta, Ohio

    Colonial Revival charm in Ohio's first permanent settlement. Rooftop gardens, historic architecture, and a welcoming innkeeper create an ideal setting for travelers and those seeking intentional community.
