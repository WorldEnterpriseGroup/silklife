# SILK Life Community Character Guide

This document contains all established characters in the SILK Life magazine universe. These are real people living in retrofitted Victorian cottages in the Mid-Ohio Valley towns of Ravenswood (WV), Parkersburg (WV), and Marietta (OH).

**Community Size:** ~50 people across 3 locations
**Setting:** 1880s-1890s Victorian cottages, retrofitted for intentional living
**Philosophy:** Y.A.C.H.T. lifestyle (Yoga, Arts, Cafe, Homes, Tech)

## Character Notes for Writers

### Voice Guidelines
- **Bill:** Short sentences. Practical observations. Ohio River metaphors.
- **Maya:** Self-deprecating about garden failures. Food-focused.
- **Elena:** Tired but warm. Night shift references. Art metaphors.
- **Tom:** Actions over words. Noticed small things. Recipe descriptions without measurements.
- **Sarah:** Hospitality language. Noticing needs before asked. Chicken anecdotes.
- **Emma:** Connector phrases ("You should meet..."). Fermentation metaphors.
- **Jacob:** Enthusiastic. Tech references. Running stats nobody asked for.
- **Rachel:** Visual descriptions (designer eye). Korean food references with mother memories.

### Seasonal Patterns
- **Spring:** Garden planning, porch reopening, Maya's seedling chaos
- **Summer:** River activities, too many tomatoes, Bill's porch gatherings peak
- **Fall:** Harvest preservation, heating system prep, Elena's Pittsburgh decision
- **Winter:** Radiator learning curves, indoor gatherings, holiday traditions

### Common Gathering Spots
1. Bill's wraparound porch (summer evenings)
2. Maya's kitchen (Saturday mornings)
3. Iris's living room (Wednesday meditation)
4. Tom's workshop (impromptu repairs)
5. Nathan's furniture shop (Marietta)
6. Tony's pizza shop (Parkersburg casual)
