Our Story

Where the SILK Brand Comes Alive

SILK Life is the heart of the SILK community - a magazine where authentic stories of intentional living, wellness, creativity, and community come together to inspire and connect. We believe that every member of our community has a story worth telling, and we're here to share those stories with the world.

Founded as part of the SILK Corp family, SILK Life serves as the editorial voice of an ecosystem that includes SILK Yoga, SILK Arts, SILK Cafe, SILK Homes, SILK Tech, and SILK Women. Together, these properties form what we call YACHT Life - a holistic approach to living that encompasses Yoga, Arts, Cafe, Homes, and Tech.

"We encourage you to consider our values and mission while creating content. Mental, physical, and spiritual wellness are at the heart of everything we do."

Our Values

Strength

Respect the universal laws to create leverage and harness the power of your entire world. Inner strength through creative expression, yoga, and personal resilience.

Integrity

Set your intentions on being a vessel of peace, serenity, and integrity. Align your life with goodness for all.

Love

Give nourishment and sensuality in all you do. Honor family as you embrace the flow of life. Compassion woven into everything.

Knowledge

Gain awareness of your surroundings and diversify skills. Absorb ancient wisdom and latest innovations.

Our Locations

SILK Life stories emerge from our intentional living communities across the Ohio Valley:

Ravenswood, WV

Four historic 1890s cottages nestled along the Ohio River. Small-town charm with walkable downtown.

Parkersburg, WV

Four 1950s mid-century modern homes. Urban homesteading with the largest victory garden in the city.

Marietta, OH

Ohio's first permanent settlement. Colonial Revival homes with river views and rooftop gardens.
