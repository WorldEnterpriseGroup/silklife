All articles follow the ORIGINAL post.html template structure including:
- Complete header with search, social icons, latest news ticker
- SILK Life logo and navigation (Home, Stories, Community, Events, About, Subscribe)
- Horizontal carousel of related articles
- Full article layout with:
  - Title, category, date, author, views, comments
  - Featured image with caption
  - Excerpt (subheadline)
  - 400-600 words of engaging body content
  - Pull quote
  - Author bio section
  - Share buttons
  - Tags and categories
  - Related articles carousel
  - Comment form
  - Sample comments
  - Sidebar with Most Read/Most Commented tabs
- Footer with SILK Life info, categories, and SILK Network links
- All required JavaScript includes
